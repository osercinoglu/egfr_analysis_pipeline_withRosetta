## The decisive result

**The new engine reproduces the prototype's stored decoys bit-for-bit.**

`checkpoints/1XKK_FMM_ckpt.pkl` holds per-decoy many-body energies for all 50 decoys.
Regenerating under `protocol="legacy"` at the same seed:

| decoy | pairs | max \|Δ\| | median \|Δ\| | Pearson r |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 1708 | 1.18e-05 | 1.06e-07 | 1.0000000000 |
| 1 | 1708 | 1.51e-05 | 8.25e-08 | 1.0000000000 |

The residual is float32 storage, as in B8. This exercises the *entire* stack — pose loading,
graph construction, region resolution, sequence draw, mutation, repack, minimisation,
backbone restore, energy extraction, many-body — and it survives a stochastic packing step,
which means the RNG consumption order is faithfully reproduced.

Getting there required matching three details exactly: the sampling alphabet is
`list(aa_freq.keys())` in **first-appearance order** (`frustration.py:329`),
`np.random.choice` is called **once per residue** rather than once for a vector, and each
mutation is applied to the pose immediately so later draws see earlier ones.

## A silent inconsistency found: sequence separation has two bases

1XKK gave **1714** pairs at the prototype's definition where the checkpoint has **1708** —
while 5GMP had reproduced its 1772 exactly.

The cause: the prototype separates by **pose index**
(`for j in range(i + seq_sep_min, n)`, `frustration.py:98`), which counts only *modelled*
residues. Across a numbering gap it treats residues that are far apart in sequence as
adjacent. Measuring by PDB numbering — the biological separation — includes those pairs.

| structure | numbering gaps | pairs by resseq | pairs by pose index |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 5GMP | 2 | 1772 | 1772 |
| 1XKK | 5 | 1714 | 1708 |

So the two definitions disagree on 6 pairs in one structure and none in another. **The
difference is structure-dependent and was entirely silent.** `contacts.seq_sep_basis` is now
an explicit analysis-stage setting (`resseq` default, `pose_index` for reproduction), and
both columns are stored so either can be selected post-hoc.

This matters less than it might: A4 established the paper states **no** sequence-separation
criterion at all, so the default is `seq_sep_min=1` and neither basis filters anything. It
matters for reproducing the prototype, and it would have quietly corrupted any comparison
between the two.

## What the switches do

`identity=native, placement=permute` is now the default — the published *shuffle*, which
conserves the amino-acid multiset exactly. A test asserts that; another asserts the
prototype's `identity=composition` i.i.d. draw does **not** conserve it, which is the
deviation A4 identified.

`scope` selects which residues change: `whole_protein` (the prototype's behaviour, ~289
residues in 1XKK) or `contact_shell` (parameter #7's "contacting residues"). The plan records
`scope` as the real deviation from the paper rather than `identity`, and both are now
expressible.

## C5: the backbone invariant is a post-condition, not a test

`assert_backbone_identical` runs on **every** decoy at 1e-6 Å, rather than being checked once
in the suite at 0.05 Å as the prototype did. A drifting backbone silently changes what the
Z-score measures, since the whole construction assumes native and decoy differ in sequence
*at fixed geometry*. A positive-control test moves an atom and confirms the assertion fires.

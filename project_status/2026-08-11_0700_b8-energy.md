The residual is float32 storage of `e_direct`/`e_fa_rep` (~7 significant digits on energies

1e-5 is negligible against a σ of order 1. Documented at the assertion.

Taken with B7's exact 1772-pair contact reproduction, the new engine is now validated
against the old one at both the graph and the energy layer, by entirely different code.

## What was built

- `EnergyEvaluator` — scores the pose **once** and holds the `EnergyGraph`. The prototype
  called `scorefxn(pose)` inside every `pairwise_energy` invocation
  (`frustration.py:167`), i.e. once per pair per background term.
- `e_fa_rep` stored **alongside** `e_direct` rather than subtracted, making
  `exclude_fa_rep` an analysis-time switch. The prototype baked it in at scoring time.
- Many-body registry over stored arrays only — no pose, no Rosetta — which is what makes
  the formula re-specifiable against a finished run and why `E_ij` is never written to disk.
  Implemented with two `bincount` passes: O(P), not O(P·degree).
- `many_body_energies_reference` — a literal, un-simplified transcription used only by
  tests, so the algebraic cancellation is *proved* rather than assumed.

## Two corrections to project documentation

**1. The plan's `pair_retained` formula was itself degenerate.** It was written as
`E_ij = e_ij + 0.5·Σ_{k∉{i,j}} e_ik + 0.5·Σ_{l∉{i,j}} e_jl` — the same exclusion
`chen_literal` makes, so it collapses identically. The non-degenerate form sums over **all**
partners: `E_ij = e_ij + 0.5·B_i + 0.5·B_j`. Corrected in §3.4, with the clean relation
`pair_retained − chen_literal == e_ij` now asserted by test.

**2. `CLAUDE.md` was wrong about edgeless pairs.** It claimed pairs beyond Rosetta's
interaction cutoff have no edge, "so some of the looser 10 Å Cα pairs carry only the
background terms". Measured on 5GMP:

| Cα–Cα | with edge | mean \|e\| |
|---|---:|---:|
| 0–6 Å | 100% | 0.80 |
| 6–12 Å | 100% | 0.55 → 0.02 |
| 12–15 Å | 94.7% | 0.016 |
| 15–20 Å | 30.6% | 0.000 |
| > 20 Å | 0% | 0.000 |

**No 10 Å Cα pair is edgeless.** Distant pairs are near-zero by *magnitude*, not by absence
of an edge. Since the superset stops at 12 Å, every pair in it is scored. `CLAUDE.md`
updated with the measurement; the test now asserts full edge coverage and will fail if the
superset is ever widened past Rosetta's graph range, where the distinction would start to
matter.
